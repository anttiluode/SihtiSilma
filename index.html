What this experiment does not claim

  
Not a new vision model
The patch memory is copied from the original SihtiImageModel BSDS500 export. This is an active-observation experiment around a fixed reconstruction, not retraining P3.

  
Not an information-gain oracle
Three nearby experts produce a variance proxy. Their probabilities are distance weights, not calibrated posteriors. Disagreement may fail to predict true error. The oracle uses hidden truth only as a retrospective bound.

  
Not yet a full closed-loop completion model
Looking replaces an observed tile with its exact pixels; the other tiles keep the original P3 prediction. The feedback policy updates its next-location score, not its reconstruction weights. Re-running Sihti completion after every measurement is a separate gate.

  
Run locally: open index.html in your browser. Assets are bundled; nothing is fetched from a server. The four photographs were distributed as held-out examples in SihtiImageModel. RGB inputs are converted to 96×64 grayscale for this inexpensive browser test, so these measurements are not comparable to the parent repo’s full-size BSDS500 benchmark.
